Help Center

Answers to the questions you ask most.

Everything you need to get started with LedgerView, understand your workflow, and get better market clarity.

How do I get started?

Create your account, verify your number, and explore live market metrics, watchlists, and research insights in minutes.

Is LedgerView suitable for options traders?

Yes. The platform includes option analytics, flow tracking, and market setup tools designed for active derivatives decisions.

Can I customize my watchlists?

Absolutely. Watchlists are built for discovery and monitoring across strategies, sectors, and positions.

Is there a mobile experience?

Yes. The interface is designed to be responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop screens for on-the-go monitoring.

Does LedgerView provide financial advice?

No. LedgerView is a research and analysis platform, and users should independently evaluate trades, sectors, and investment choices.

How do I contact support?

Reach out to support@ledgerview.in or visit the contact page for product support, onboarding, and compliance queries.
